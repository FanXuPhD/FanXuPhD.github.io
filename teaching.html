Selected Teaching Experience

Selected courses in educational technology, online learning, and instructional design.

MSET 365: Technology Integration for Effective Instruction

University of New Mexico · Online · Fall 2025, Spring 2026, Fall 2026

An undergraduate course preparing future educators to make thoughtful decisions about technology integration in K–12 classrooms. Students explore instructional design frameworks, accessibility and Universal Design for Learning, technology-supported assessment, and strategies for creating engaging learning experiences. Recent course activities also examine AI literacy, responsible uses of generative AI, and how teachers can preserve student agency while using emerging tools. Through lesson planning, hands-on design projects, and a teaching showcase, students connect educational theory with practical classroom decisions.

ESLTECH 2011: Keys to Academic Success for Online Learners

The Ohio State University · Online · Fall 2023

An undergraduate course focused on helping students develop the knowledge and strategies needed to succeed in online learning environments. Topics include navigating digital learning spaces, managing time and academic responsibilities, communicating and collaborating online, and becoming more independent and reflective learners. The course emphasizes practical habits and digital learning skills that students can apply across their academic work.

ESLTECH 8295: Applied Instructional Design

The Ohio State University · Hybrid · Fall 2022

A graduate-level course introducing the application of instructional design theories and processes to authentic educational challenges. Students consider how to analyze learners and learning contexts, articulate objectives, align instructional activities with assessment, and evaluate design decisions. The course emphasizes connecting research-informed design principles with practical solutions for learning in face-to-face, online, and blended environments.
