I am an Assistant Professor of Educational Technology in the Department of Teacher Education, Educational Leadership & Policy at the University of New Mexico. My research interests include AI in education, learning design, human–AI collaboration, and computational thinking. I am especially interested in designing learning experiences that support educators’ professional judgment and learners’ agency across diverse educational contexts, including STEM education and teacher professional learning.

My current work emphasizes community-engaged, impact-oriented research that connects educational research with the needs and experiences of teachers, students, families, and local communities. Through the NSF-supported New Mexico TODOS project, I collaborate on efforts to integrate AI literacy and responsible AI use into STEM teacher preparation and professional learning. My Future Ready Educators project focuses on culturally responsive, scenario-based professional learning that helps teachers make informed decisions about when, why, and how to use AI in their classrooms.

I welcome conversations and collaborations around community-engaged research, AI literacy, teacher professional learning, and learning design.

My broader scholarship explores how people learn with and through emerging technologies, and how learning design can turn research insights into meaningful educational experiences. My doctoral research examined individual factors and mutual engagement in middle-school pair programming; it continues to inform my interest in collaboration, computational thinking, and learner-centered design.

I received my Ph.D. in Learning Technologies from The Ohio State University (2023), an M.S. in Information Technology in Education from the University of Hong Kong (2018), and a B.S. in Educational Technology from Shanghai Normal University (2017). Before joining UNM in 2025, I worked as a Senior Learning Designer at Ohio State’s Center on Education and Training for Employment.
