Building Robotic Infrastructure for Embodied AI: A Humanoid Robot for UNM

This interdisciplinary initiative brings together researchers in engineering, computer science, education, and the arts to expand the University of New Mexico’s capacity for embodied AI research. A humanoid robot provides a shared platform for exploring how intelligent systems perceive, act, and interact with people in physical environments.

Beyond technical research, the project creates opportunities to examine human–robot interaction, trust, creative expression, and educational applications of emerging robotics. My involvement centers on the educational potential of embodied AI, including opportunities to make AI concepts more tangible and engaging for K–12 and higher education learners.
