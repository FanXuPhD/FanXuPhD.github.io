PROOF-AI: A Process-Oriented Reasoning Framework for AI-Integrated Undergraduate Computer Science Education

PROOF-AI explores how generative AI can support undergraduate computer science students’ reasoning without taking over the intellectual work of learning. As AI tools become more common in programming and problem solving, the project asks how educational experiences can keep students actively involved in understanding, explaining, and evaluating their own solutions.

The project takes a process-oriented, human-centered perspective on AI-integrated computing education, with attention to critical thinking, problem-solving practices, and students’ capacity to judge the quality of AI assistance. Its broader goal is to inform learning designs that balance access to powerful tools with independent reasoning and responsible use.
