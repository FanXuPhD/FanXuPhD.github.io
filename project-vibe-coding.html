Vibe Coding for Learning-by-Design: Integrating AI-Supported Creative Coding into Teacher Preparation

This project investigates vibe coding as a creative, AI-supported approach to learning-by-design in teacher preparation. By describing ideas in natural language and iteratively working with generative AI, future educators can build interactive learning artifacts without needing extensive prior programming experience. The emphasis is on instructional purpose, design reasoning, and meaningful learner experiences—not simply producing working code.

In course-based design activities, teacher candidates explore how playful, interactive artifacts might help learners make choices, receive feedback, and engage with subject matter. They develop prototypes, test and refine their ideas, and reflect on how AI shapes the design process and their own professional agency. The project considers both the opportunities for creative participation and the need to critically evaluate AI-generated outputs.
