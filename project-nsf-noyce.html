Robert Noyce Teacher Scholarship Program Track 3 at the University of New Mexico

Learn more about the project at nmtodos.org ↗

The NSF-supported Robert Noyce Teacher Scholarship Program Track 3 at the University of New Mexico, known as New Mexico TODOS, brings together university faculty, educators, and community partners to strengthen STEM teacher preparation, professional learning, and educational opportunities across New Mexico. The initiative emphasizes sustained collaboration and teacher leadership in responding to the needs of local schools and communities.

Building on this work, related AI literacy efforts support educators in understanding the possibilities, limitations, and responsibilities of AI in STEM teaching and learning. The work seeks to connect responsible AI use with teachers’ instructional expertise, students’ inquiry, and equitable participation, while creating opportunities for educators to learn with and from one another.
